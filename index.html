Privacy Policy

SnapPDF: PDF Editor & Scanner

Effective date: October 22, 2026 · Last updated: October 22, 2026
Android package: com.techtyphoon.snappdf

SnapPDF processes your PDFs, images, scans and edits on your device. TechTyphoon does not receive these files through the app. Some features use Google components or other apps you choose; their data practices are explained below.

1. About this policy

TechTyphoon develops SnapPDF. This policy explains how the Android app handles information and how this privacy-policy website is hosted. It describes the current release, version 0.2.1. For privacy questions, contact TechTyphoon.html@gmail.com.

2. Information processed on your device

SnapPDF accesses documents and images that you select, share into the app, capture or create. Depending on the tools you use, this can include PDF contents, photos, scanned pages, recognized text, form entries, annotations, handwritten signatures and document passwords supplied to open or protect files. This information is used to perform your requested operations locally.

The app stores imported and generated files in its private storage. Its local library index records file names, sizes, content hashes, folders, favorites, timestamps and operation history to organize your documents. Local settings include appearance, image quality, OCR language/script and an optional annotation author name. An author name or other metadata may become part of a document you export.

SnapPDF does not require an account. The current app contains no advertising SDK, developer analytics service, remote crash-reporting service, cloud document sync or developer-operated upload endpoint. TechTyphoon does not receive your document contents, passwords or local library history through these app features, and does not sell this local information.

3. On-device processing and Google components

PDF viewing and editing use PDFium through pdfrx and PDFBox-Android. Image tools process files locally. SnapPDF uses bundled Google ML Kit text-recognition models for Latin and Devanagari scripts, and ML Kit selfie segmentation for portrait background processing. Segmentation separates the person from the background; SnapPDF does not use it to identify people.

Document scanning uses the Google Play services ML Kit document scanner. Google Play services may need an Internet connection to download its scanner components. Google describes ML Kit input and output processing, including the document-scanning flow, as on-device; document images and recognition results are not sent to Google servers by these ML Kit APIs. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy and the document scanner documentation.

Google documents SDK diagnostics and usage metrics such as device and app information, installation identifiers, performance, configuration, events and errors. These support operation, diagnostics and improvement of its APIs. SnapPDF's release manifest removes Internet and network-state permissions, restricting direct network access by code in the app's process. This does not disable the separate Google Play services application's network access. Google components may communicate under their own permissions and policies; we do not claim that their diagnostics are disabled. See Google's ML Kit data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy for their processing and controls.

4. Permissions and access

  • Documents and folders: Android's system file picker grants access to files or folders you choose. SnapPDF does not request broad storage or all-files access. A selected export folder may retain an Android access grant for later use.
  • Camera and scans: SnapPDF launches a camera app or the Google Play services scanner. SnapPDF itself does not request the camera permission; the selected camera app or Google Play services handles camera access.
  • Other personal information: SnapPDF does not request contacts, location, microphone, phone, SMS or advertising-ID permissions.

The release app has no Internet permission. Access through an external file provider, scanner, camera, browser, sharing destination or print service is governed by that service's permissions and privacy practices.

5. Sharing, exporting and external services

When you choose Save, Export, Share or Print, SnapPDF passes the selected output to the destination or Android service you choose. A cloud storage provider, messaging app or print service may upload or retain it. Opening an external link, if handled by another app, is also subject to that app's policy. These actions can disclose the file's contents and any remaining metadata, including signatures, author names or image metadata. Review documents before sharing.

SnapPDF does not automatically upload your library to TechTyphoon. Files you send outside the app are controlled by you and the receiving service; deleting a local copy does not delete those external copies.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

Library files and preferences remain on your device until removed. Files moved to Trash can be restored or permanently deleted. The app removes trash entries older than 30 days when its library loads; this is not a continuously running deletion service. Saved signatures can be deleted from the signature library.

Temporary files and previews may remain in app-private cache between operations. Startup cleanup removes older working files, using 12-hour or 24-hour thresholds depending on the cache area. Settings also provides a temporary-file cleanup action. Cleanup runs when the app executes it; it is not guaranteed at the exact age threshold.

Clearing SnapPDF's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes its app-private data under Android's normal behavior. Copies exported to other folders, cloud services, printers or recipients remain until you remove them there. App backup and device-transfer exclusions are configured for its private data.

7. Security

SnapPDF uses Android app-private storage and scoped file-sharing permissions. Saved signature-library entries are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key managed by Android Keystore. Ordinary library documents and temporary working files are not all separately encrypted by SnapPDF. Password protection applies to PDF files when you explicitly use that tool. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure; protect access to your device and exported documents.

8. Contacting TechTyphoon and your choices

If you email TechTyphoon.html@gmail.com, you voluntarily send your email address, message and any attachments through email providers. This is separate from local app processing. Please do not send passwords or sensitive documents when a description of the issue will suffice.

You can control local information by selecting which files to import, changing settings, deleting files or signatures, emptying Trash, clearing app storage and managing Android permissions. TechTyphoon cannot retrieve or delete documents held only on your device. You may contact the email above to ask about personal information you have directly sent to TechTyphoon, including access, correction or deletion requests. Rights concerning information held by Google, GitHub or other services are handled through those providers and their policies.

9. Children’s privacy

SnapPDF has no account-registration flow and does not ask users to submit their age or other personal profile information. The same local-processing behavior applies regardless of age. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to TechTyphoon through the contact address, contact us about its removal. The Google components and external services described above have their own privacy policies.

Visiting a linked website or sending an email is subject to the destination provider's practices.
